## Executive Result

At the cutoff, the repository supports three different conclusions:

1. **PIPELINE WORKS / DATA QUALITY GOOD, narrowly.** The 2026-09-16 Q9 day was
   valid: 489 complete P/A/B/C windows, full-session coverage, no missing
   selected candidate, and 99.82% usable forward coverage. The trade artifact
   inventory was 8/8 required artifacts. This validates that day's measurement
   pipeline, not the economic quality of its candidates.
2. **DISCRIMINATORS exist in retrospective data, but no general live edge is
   confirmed.** The strongest recurring pattern is short-horizon, early,
   directional common-stock movement. It is explicitly subject to post-hoc and
   market-snapshot coverage limitations. Broad Rank-1, broad HIGH-risk, broad
   opening, broad BTC bullishness, broad longer holding, runner-up cascade, and
   broad guard relaxation are not supported.
3. **No exact candidate is LIVE-TRADE READY from this evidence.** The only
   positive opening candidate (`IMMEDIATE_OPENING_PROBE`) had favorable
   cumulative retrospective/prospective-looking data but failed its fixed
   runtime validation. Q10 and Q12 remained under-sampled; Q11 and Q9 had
   primary data-authority blockers. Strategist and feedback causal contribution
   was not measurable.

## Top Findings

1. **The strongest confirmed negative rule is no automatic runner-up cascade.**
   Q8 runner-up outcomes were approximately flat at +5m/+15m and negative at
   +30m/+60m. This is a defensive policy result, not an alpha source.
   Source: `docs/evaluation/q8_final_comprehensive_review_2026-06-20.md`.
2. **Broad VWAP-reclaim/pullback relaxation is not supported.** Q8's largest
   VWAP-reclaim lane was negative from +5m through +60m. The later directional
   breadth split says candidate composition matters, not that broad relaxation
   should occur. Sources: Q8 final review; `docs/offline_alpha/deep_discriminator_squeeze_2026-08-24.md`.
3. **Rank-1 contains short-horizon information but is not a tradable rule by
   itself.** Canonical N=107 peaks at +15m/+30m and falls negative by EOD;
   Board's broad `OPEN_0_20_RANK1_30M` has N=137 but only +0.3289% / PF 1.2265.
   Sources: deep discriminator review; 9/16 Board JSON.
4. **The most promising structure is early common-stock directionality, not
   HIGH risk or opening time alone.** Historical HIGH-common results were
   strong, while leveraged/inverse ETF HIGH cohorts were weak. However, the
   Board's forward/prospective candidate was negative and market snapshot
   coverage was incomplete. This remains a hypothesis, not a promotion.
5. **Immediate opening has a material discrepancy that blocks promotion.**
   Its cumulative N=29 looked strong, but its predefined five-session runtime
   validation returned `FAIL_RUNTIME_EFFECT`. Fixed validation outranks the
   exploratory/cumulative result. Source: `immediate_opening_runtime_validation.json`.
6. **Holding longer is not the answer.** The broad Rank-1 sample loses edge by
   EOD and later multi-day averages are contributor-driven. Short-lived alpha
   should not be converted into universal overnight/long-hold policy.
7. **Q10, Q11, and Q12 do not have enough clean evidence to support trading
   behavior.** Q10 only has three prospective days and 17 outcomes; Q12's
   qualifying Board cohort is zero and key 9/16 inputs were missing; Q11's
   primary authority was blocked.
8. **Stage-2 Strategist refresh is the only agent layer with a measurable
   directional warning:** 806 same-universe comparisons, -0.4470 pp average
   delta. It is not enough to conclude the entire Strategist is harmful.
9. **Feedback/memory has no measurable ROI at all.** 6,832 recorded exposures,
   zero consumption/adoption. It is currently a stored observation system,
   not a demonstrated learning loop.
10. **Daily integrity does not resolve causality.** On 9/16, Q9 had excellent
    linkage/coverage, but one realized trade cannot identify Scanner, Monitor,
    Commander, or horizon responsibility.

## Data Quality and Contamination Audit

### Good / usable at the stated scope

- Q9 2026-09-16: complete P/A/B/C linkage 489/489, selected-candidate missing
  count 0, usable forward coverage 99.82%, session valid.
- Daily trade artifact inventory: 8/8 required artifacts present.
- Short-alpha join: opening episodes 137, feature episodes 202, joined 137,
  missing join count 0.
- Board integrity: PASS; historical/prospective separation explicitly enabled.

### Material limitations

- Q8's old cost profile had a permanently sticky 6.5539% conservative cost
  floor. Its guard-frequency and entry-scarcity conclusions cannot be read as
  clean cost-policy evidence.
- Q10 9/16 had unusable KOSPI/KOSDAQ late checkpoints and close values, so its
  index return comparisons were partial rather than complete.
- Q12 on 9/16 had missing BTC 08:55 return, surge state, and breakout state;
  a negative Woori gap cannot substitute for those inputs.
- Q13/Q14 labels can be outcome-conditioned. `Scanner Ranking Failure` in the
  daily Q14 report is explicitly `causal_eligible=false`.
- Agent-effectiveness daily reports require observations across at least two
  days and an appropriate counterfactual. High window count on one day does
  not satisfy that requirement.
- Historical discovery, prospective shadow, and fixed runtime validation are
  separate evidence classes. They must not be pooled.

## 2026-09-16 RESEARCH STATE

### WHAT WE KNOW

- Automatically entering every opening Rank-1 is invalid; the broad control
  fails concentration/robustness checks.
- Automatically selecting a runner-up after a blocked Top-1 is invalid.
- Broad VWAP reclaim and broad pullback relaxation are invalid.
- Broad EOD/multi-day holding of Rank-1 selections is invalid.
- Q9's 9/16 measurement run was structurally complete; this says nothing about
  whether its one realized trade was economically representative.

### WHAT WE SUSPECT

- A narrow early common-stock directional cohort may have short-horizon alpha.
- `DIRECTIONAL_BREADTH` may separate useful VWAP pullback candidates from
  liquidity-led candidates.
- Strong BTC plus local Woori confirmation may be different from ordinary BTC
  bullishness, with a +30m rather than immediate horizon.
- Stage-2 Strategist refresh may add complexity without improving outcomes.

### WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW

- Whether any of the above survives a fixed, sufficient, multi-regime,
  prospective window after costs.
- Whether the full Strategist provides positive or negative net value.
- Whether Reporter feedback or memory changes any later decision or outcome.
- Whether Q10 pre-open US signals forecast Korean large caps/indices with
  decision-grade reliability.
- Whether Q11 has a valid primary outcome series.

### WHAT WE SHOULD DO NEXT

1. Do not add a new evaluation axis or alter trade behavior from this review.
2. Close the identified authority and point-in-time input gaps first.
3. Continue only the already-fixed prospective observations for the few active
   candidates, retaining separate evidence classes.
4. At the next review, require the program matrix fields above and select at
   most one behavior change only if an exact candidate passes its predeclared
   contract.
